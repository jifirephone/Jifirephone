# Beat-cut storefront reel (2026-11-13)
Owner sent two screen recordings of a live-action "seamless transitions" reel (whip pans, cover wipes, B&W hits, cuts on a ~130 BPM beat)
and asked for the same kind of video/transitions/tune, built on the shop: storefront photo → website → phones → delivery.
Technique only. We have stills, not live footage, so the transitions are done on stills:
punch-zooms on the storefront photo (wide → sign → brand signs → door), zoom-through the door with a flash into the real site in a phone,
six phones on whip pans (one per 2 beats), an object fly-wipe into «خصم 25,000 / jifirecode», delivery route (المحل → بيتك) «توصيل مجاني لكل العراق»,
stamp «مضمون» (سنة للجديد · شهر للمستعمل، مع وصل ضمان), back to the storefront with the address, end card.
Music is our own synthesized 130 BPM beat (`scripts/make_beat.py`), never the reference's track; every cut sits on the beat grid.
The reference's «Comment "PANDA"» box is not used (owner rule). `remotion/src/disc25/StoreBeat.tsx`, 25.6 s, −14 LUFS.
